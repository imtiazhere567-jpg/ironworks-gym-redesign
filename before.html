Welcome to our Website

Welcome to the official website of Ironworks Gym. Established in 2009, Ironworks Gym has been serving the local community for many years and we pride ourselves on our friendly atmosphere and excellent facilities. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced athlete we have something for everyone. Our experienced and qualified staff are always on hand to help you achieve your fitness goals. Please feel free to browse our website to find out more about what we offer. We are open from early morning until late evening seven days a week (please see opening hours section or call reception for bank holiday times).

Our Classes

  • Spin - An indoor cycling class suitable for all levels of fitness. Please bring a towel and water bottle.
  • HIIT - High Intensity Interval Training. Please consult your GP before taking part.
  • Yoga - A relaxing class to improve flexibility. Mats provided.
