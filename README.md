# Ironworks Gym — Before & After

A website-redesign demo for the short video *"Guess what people do when a pop-up covers your site"*.
Ironworks Gym is a sample business; photos are from Unsplash and videos from Mixkit.

- `index.html` — start page with Before / After cards
- `before.html` — the old 2014-style site, fully covered by a newsletter pop-up with a tiny 8px close button
- `after.html` — the redesign: no pop-up, scroll-driven animations, timetable booking and a join flow
  (`after.html?popup=scroll` shows a polite pop-up that waits until the visitor scrolls)
- `compare.html` — old vs new side by side in phone frames
- `search.html`, `kit.html` — extra assets for recording the video

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step; open `index.html` or serve the folder.
